# Deep dive: Automate my week (about 4 minutes)

Host: Ada Lovelace (a cameo if she's on the bench).

Pitch: "Tell me the chores you repeat. I'll build small engines for the
boring ones, and tell you which to keep doing by hand."

Questions:
1. What do you do by hand every week that feels like a machine could do it?
   (buttons: Checking a website / Sending the same message / Updating a
   list or spreadsheet / Paying or tracking bills)
2. Anything you check and forget, or remember too late? (a delivery, a
   price, a school portal, a renewal)
3. How do you want to hear from your engines? (buttons: Only when
   something changes / A line in the morning brief / Weekly summary)

Set up before you finish:
- notes/automations.md (create it): each engine, what it does, when it runs,
  how to stop it ("say 'stop the X engine'").
- Up to two engines now, each approved first: a watcher (the `watchers`
  skill), a scheduled reminder or report (cronjob tool), or a reusable
  template saved to the notes.
- Anything rare or personal (twice a year, messages to family): say why
  it's better by hand, and leave it.
- No automation ever sends a message, spends or books by itself: it
  prepares, then asks.

Proof moment: build the first engine on the spot and show its first result
(today's price, the page as it is now, the drafted weekly message).

Saves to: automations.md.
Careful: one engine at a time; never connect accounts or install anything
without a yes. Offer to seat Ada if they liked this.
