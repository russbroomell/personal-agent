# Deep dive: Home & car (about 4 minutes)

Pitch: "Houses and cars are a pile of small deadlines nobody tracks: filters,
inspections, registrations, warranties. Give me 4 minutes and I'll track them
all and nudge you before they bite."

Questions (one at a time; park anything skipped):
1. Own or rent? (buttons: Own / Rent / It's complicated)
2. What's the oldest thing in the house you're worried about? (furnace,
   water heater, roof, appliances)
3. Any regular maintenance you know you're behind on? (buttons: HVAC filter
   / Gutters / Smoke alarms / Nothing, I'm a saint)
4. Car(s): year, make, model, roughly how many miles. When is the
   registration due, and when was the last oil change?
5. Who do you call for repairs (plumber, electrician, mechanic)? Any you'd
   rather never call again?
6. Anything under warranty you'd hate to miss? (Receipts can come later as
   photos.)

Set up before you finish (confirm each in one line):
- A maintenance calendar in home.md: each item, how often, next due date.
- Open loops for anything overdue, with nudge dates.
- Reminders 2 weeks before registration, inspection and warranty end dates
  (one scheduled job per date, or add them to the morning brief's due list).
- A watcher for a recall check on their car (official recall lookup page,
  monthly), if they agree.

Proof moment: look up the right HVAC filter size or the next service
interval for their car model, or find a well-reviewed local pro with
Saturday hours.

Saves to: home.md (house, car, providers, warranties), open-loops.md.
