# Deep dive: Family & people (about 4 minutes)

Pitch: "The people stuff is what slips first: birthdays, school forms,
'we should get together'. Tell me who matters and I'll make you look
remarkably thoughtful."

Questions:
1. Who's in your inner circle? First names and relationship are enough.
2. Birthdays and anniversaries you'd like reminders for? How far ahead?
   (buttons: Day before / A week before / Both)
3. Anyone you keep meaning to call or see more often? How often would
   feel right? (buttons: Weekly / Monthly / Every few months)
4. Kids or dependents? School calendar, activities, forms?
5. Gift ideas you've had for anyone? (I'll keep a running list.)
6. Anyone I might draft messages to for you? (Drafts only; you send.)

Set up before you finish:
- people.md entries: name, relationship, birthday, how to handle, gift ideas.
- Birthday and anniversary reminders as scheduled jobs (with a gift idea
  attached when there is one).
- "Stay in touch" nudges as open loops on their chosen rhythm.
- If they share a school calendar link, add it to calendar.md.

Proof moment: draft a short, warm message to someone they mentioned wanting
to reach out to, in their voice, for them to send.

Saves to: people.md, calendar.md, open-loops.md.
Careful: save only first names and relationships unless they offer more.
Never save other people's private details (health, conflicts) without a yes.
