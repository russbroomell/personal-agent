# Deep dive: Work & inbox (about 5 minutes)

Pitch: "Most inboxes are 10% things that need you and 90% noise. Tell me how
your work runs and I'll sort the 10% to the top, prep your meetings and
catch the promises you make."

Questions:
1. What do you do, and what does a heavy week look like?
2. Who are your VIPs: people whose emails always matter? (names or domains)
3. What counts as noise? (buttons: Newsletters / Notifications / Sales
   pitches / All of the above)
4. Do you want a twice-daily triage? (buttons: Morning + afternoon / Morning
   only / Only when I forward)
5. Which meetings deserve a prep note? (buttons: External only / Anything
   with a new person / All / None)
6. Do you make promises by email you sometimes forget? ("I'll get back to
   you Thursday")

Set up before you finish:
- VIPs and noise rules in a work.md note (create it).
- Triage schedule using the `inbox-triage` skill (works on forwarded mail
  until a mailbox is connected).
- Meeting prep: if a calendar link is in calendar.md, add "flag meetings
  needing prep" to the morning brief.
- Follow-up tracking: promises go into open-loops.md as "theirs".

Proof moment: ask them to forward one messy email thread right now; triage
it and draft a reply for approval.

Saves to: work.md, people.md, open-loops.md.
Careful: drafts only; never send as them without a yes for that message.
