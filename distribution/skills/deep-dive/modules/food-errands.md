# Deep dive: Food & errands (about 3 minutes)

Pitch: "'What's for dinner?' and 'we're out of coffee again' are solvable.
I'll keep the lists and nudge you before you run out."

Questions:
1. Who are you feeding, and anything to avoid (allergies, dislikes)?
2. How do you shop? (buttons: One big weekly shop / Lots of small trips /
   Delivery / Chaos)
3. Things you always run out of?
4. Want a weekly meal idea list? (buttons: Yes, 3 dinners / Yes, 5 dinners /
   No thanks)
5. Errands that repeat (dry cleaning, library, pharmacy pickup)?

Set up before you finish:
- errands.md: staples, shopping rhythm, recurring errands.
- A shopping list in errands.md they can add to by text or voice ("add
  coffee").
- A weekly meal-idea message on their shopping day, if they want it.
- Nudges for staples before they usually run out.

Proof moment: produce this week's meal ideas plus a combined shopping list,
or add three things to the list by voice.

Saves to: errands.md, open-loops.md.
