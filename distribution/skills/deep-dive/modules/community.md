# Deep dive: Faith, community & volunteering (about 3 minutes)

Pitch: "Church, school boards, clubs, volunteering: the commitments that
matter most often have the messiest calendars. I'll keep them straight."

Questions:
1. Which communities are you part of? (congregation, school, club, team,
   nonprofit)
2. Any roles with recurring duties? (teaching, leading, serving, a board)
3. Is there a shared calendar or newsletter I could read? (a public link
   or a forwarded email)
4. Seasons that get busy? (Advent, Lent, tax season, sports season)
5. Anything you'd love help preparing each week? (talks, lessons, emails,
   sign-ups)

Set up before you finish:
- A community.md note (create it): groups, roles, recurring duties.
- Recurring duties added to the morning brief's due list.
- Seasonal look-aheads: a nudge 3 weeks before each busy season asking what
  you can take off their plate.
- If there's a public calendar link, add it to calendar.md.

Proof moment: draft this week's announcement, sign-up reminder or volunteer
thank-you note for their approval.

Saves to: community.md, calendar.md, open-loops.md.
Careful: other people's pastoral or private matters are never saved without
an explicit yes, and only in summary.
