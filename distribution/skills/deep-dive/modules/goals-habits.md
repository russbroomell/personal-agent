# Deep dive: Goals & habits (about 3 minutes)

Pitch: "Pick one or two things you want to do more of. I'll check in at the
right moments, keep score honestly, and never nag."

Questions:
1. One or two things you'd like to do more consistently? (reading,
   exercise, sleep, learning, a side project)
2. What does "good" look like? (e.g. "3 runs a week", "20 pages a night")
3. When do you usually slip? (buttons: Weekends / Busy weeks / Evenings /
   No pattern)
4. How should I check in? (buttons: Daily nudge / Weekly score / Only if I
   fall behind)

Set up before you finish:
- A goals.md note (create it) with each goal, target and check-in style.
- Scheduled check-ins at their chosen rhythm; a weekly score in the Friday
  recap.
- A rule: if they say they're tired, sick or overwhelmed, pause check-ins
  for that week without asking twice.

Proof moment: set the first check-in for tomorrow and say exactly when it
will come and what it will ask.

Saves to: goals.md.
Careful: no weight, food or appearance commentary unless they explicitly ask
for it. Never shame. Rex will want to run this one; let him.
