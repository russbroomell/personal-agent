# Deep dive: Health & appointments (about 3 minutes)

Pitch: "I won't give medical advice, but I'm great at the logistics: when
checkups are due, refills, and the phone tag of booking them."

Before you start: "This one's personal. Everything stays on your server, and
I'll ask before remembering anything specific. Want to do it?" (buttons:
Yes / Skip for now). If they skip, park the whole module.

Questions:
1. When were your last checkup, dentist and eye exam, roughly? (buttons for
   each: This year / Last year / Longer / Not sure)
2. Any regular prescriptions that need refill reminders? Name and roughly
   how often; no need for doses unless you want them.
3. Anyone else in the household whose appointments you manage?
4. Do you prefer to book by phone, online, or have me draft the message?

Set up before you finish:
- A checkup cadence in a health.md note (create it), with next-due dates.
- Refill reminders as scheduled jobs a few days before each runs out.
- Open loops for anything overdue, with a gentle nudge date.

Proof moment: find the booking page or phone number for their dentist or
doctor's office and offer to draft the booking message.

Saves to: health.md (create; mark it private), open-loops.md.
Careful: never diagnose or advise on treatment. Ask before saving any
condition or medication. Health topics are serious: personality strength
drops to 1 for this module.
