# Meeting prep

1. Find the meeting (calendar link in calendar.md, or what they told you).
2. Look up each attendee in people.md and search past chats for them.
   Public info only if they ask (a company website, a public profile).
3. Reply with at most 6 lines:
   - Who: one line per person (role, how you know them).
   - Last time: the last thing discussed and when.
   - Promised: anything either side committed to, and whether it's done.
   - Raise: one suggestion for what to bring up.
4. Offer: "Want me to remind you 15 minutes before?" If yes, create a
   one-off scheduled job.
5. Afterwards, if they tell you how it went, update people.md and add any
   new commitments to open-loops.md.

Never guess facts about people. "I don't have notes on Dana yet" is fine.
