# Deep dive: Small business (about 5 minutes)

Pitch: "If you run something yourself, I can be the back office: chasing
invoices, following up leads, and keeping clients from falling through the
cracks."

Questions:
1. What's the business, in a sentence? Who are your customers?
2. How do new customers find you, and what happens next? (buttons: Email /
   Phone / Website form / Referrals)
3. Do you chase late invoices yourself? How many are usually outstanding?
4. Any recurring admin you hate? (quotes, scheduling, reminders, reviews,
   social posts)
5. What tools do you use? (calendar, invoicing, a spreadsheet, a notebook)

Set up before you finish:
- A business.md note (create it): offer, customers, pipeline steps, tools.
- A lightweight lead and client list in business.md, with "last contact"
  and "next step".
- Follow-up nudges for leads that go quiet (open loops, 3 and 7 days).
- Invoice chasing: a list of outstanding invoices (client, amount, due date)
  and polite reminder drafts at 7 and 14 days overdue, for their approval.

Proof moment: draft a friendly payment reminder for one late invoice, or a
follow-up to a lead that went quiet.

Saves to: business.md, people.md, open-loops.md.
Careful: drafts only. No customer payment details. Their customers' private
information is theirs; save only what the work needs.
