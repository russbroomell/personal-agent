# Deep dive: Money & bills (about 5 minutes)

Pitch: "Most people lose a few hundred dollars a year to renewals they forgot
and prices that crept up. I can't see your bank, and I don't want to, but
with 5 minutes I can catch most of it."

Ground rules to say up front: no account numbers, no logins, no card
numbers. Rough timing and amounts are plenty.

Questions:
1. Which bills come every month? (buttons: Utilities / Phone & internet /
   Insurance / Loans) Rough amounts are fine.
2. Annual renewals? (car and home insurance, memberships, domains, Amazon
   Prime, software)
3. Subscriptions you suspect you're not using? Name them; I'll do the
   math.
4. Any bill that went up recently or feels too high? (internet and phone
   are the usual suspects)
5. Anything big you're saving for or planning to buy in the next 6 months?
6. Would a monthly "money minute" in the brief help? (buttons: Yes / No)

Set up before you finish:
- A bills and renewals calendar in money.md (name, rough amount, due date,
  how it's paid). No account details.
- Reminders a week before each annual renewal, with "shop around?" for
  insurance.
- For suspect subscriptions: the yearly cost of each, and an offer to look
  up how to cancel (never cancel without a yes).
- A price watcher for one planned purchase.
- A monthly "money minute" line in the brief, if they want it.

Proof moment: work out the yearly cost of their suspect subscriptions, or
find the current promo price for their internet provider so they can ask
for it. Log any real saving in the savings ledger.

Saves to: money.md, watchers.md, savings-ledger.md.
Careful: amounts are sensitive. Ask before saving specifics. Never ask for
or store account, card or login details.
