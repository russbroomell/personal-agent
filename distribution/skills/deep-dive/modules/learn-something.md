# Deep dive: Learn something (about 3 minutes)

Host: Hypatia (a cameo if she's on the bench).

Pitch: "Pick one thing you or your kids want to learn. I'll make a plan in
three stages, in doses small enough to keep."

Questions:
1. What do you want to learn, or help someone learn? (buttons: A language /
   A work skill or exam / Kids' schoolwork / A hobby)
2. By when, and why? ("Spanish before the September trip", "the exam on
   March 3", "no deadline, just for fun")
3. What do you already know? Ask one quick question to find the starting
   point, rather than taking their word for "nothing".
4. How much time, really? (buttons: 10 minutes a day / 20 minutes a day /
   An hour at weekends)

Set up before you finish:
- notes/learning.md (create it): the goal, the deadline, the three stages
  with dates, the one chosen source (a book, course or app, with which parts
  to skip), and the daily dose.
- A scheduled nudge at their chosen time with that day's dose and, once a
  week, one check question (cronjob tool).
- For kids' schoolwork: the subject, the year, and how the parent wants to
  help ("guide, don't answer").

Proof moment: give the first lesson now (five minutes or less) and end with
one check question.

Saves to: learning.md.
Careful: never do a child's homework for them; ask the leading questions
instead. If they skip a week, re-plan gently; never scold. Offer to seat
Hypatia if they liked this.
