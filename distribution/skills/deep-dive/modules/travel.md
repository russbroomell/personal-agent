# Deep dive: Travel (about 3 minutes)

Pitch: "Trips are 40 small tasks hiding behind one fun one. I'll watch
prices, flights and check-in times, and keep the packing list."

Questions:
1. Any trips coming up in the next few months? Where and when?
2. Home airport(s), and how you like to fly? (buttons: Cheapest / Fewest
   stops / Best times / Points matter)
3. Seat, hotel or car preferences worth remembering?
4. Do you forget the same things every trip? (chargers, meds, passport)
5. Want a price watcher on a trip you haven't booked yet?

Set up before you finish:
- Travel preferences in a travel.md note (create it).
- For each upcoming trip: an entry in calendar.md and open loops for
  booking steps not done.
- A fare or hotel price watcher for an unbooked trip.
- A packing-list template in travel.md, sent automatically 2 days before
  each trip.
- On travel days: flight-status watcher every 2 hours, if they share the
  flight number.

Proof moment: check fares for one upcoming trip and report the best option
against their preferences, or build their packing list now.

Saves to: travel.md, calendar.md, watchers.md, open-loops.md.
Careful: no passport numbers, loyalty passwords or card details. Never book
without a yes.
