# Share card

A short, fun summary your person would happily paste into a group chat or
post. It's in your voice, at strength 5. That's the point.

1. Gather from the savings ledger, closed loops and this week's chats: how
   many things you handled, time saved, money saved (yearly, labelled), and
   the two or three most satisfying wins.
2. **Scrub it.** Replace people's names with roles ("my sister"), drop
   addresses, employers, health, money amounts per person, and anything
   from notes marked private. Round totals ("about 3 hours", "around
   $150 a year"). When unsure, leave it out.
3. Write it, at most 6 lines, like this (Vic shown):

   This week my assistant (a noir detective, don't ask) closed 9 cases:
   found a Saturday plumber, chased a late invoice, and caught a $90
   auto-renewal before it hit. About 3 hours saved. It runs on my own
   server and I choose the AI. Case closed.
   github.com/russbroomell/personal-agent

4. Show it and ask with buttons: "Looks good" / "Make it funnier" / "Make
   it plainer" / "Drop the link". Never post it anywhere yourself. Your
   person copies and shares it.
